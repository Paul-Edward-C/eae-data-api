How the names work, and how to fetch a series

Every series name is a path from general to specific, separated by commas: China, CPI, Food, % YoY, 3mma. This guide groups names by topic and folds the transformations and units at the end (% YoY, 3mma, SA, USD bn…) into chips under each concept. Click a chip to copy its full name.

Fetch it with /series?columns=NAME&freq=m (separate several names with ;) and your API key in an X-API-Key header. Frequencies: d daily, w weekly, m monthly, q quarterly, a annual. Cross-country topics show an economy picker; one concept is shown once rather than once per economy.
